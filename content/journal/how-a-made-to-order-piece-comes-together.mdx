A made-to-order piece is built for one person, one room. Here is what that journey looks like.

## 1. You tell us what you have in mind

Start on the [custom order page](/custom). Tell us what you want, the size, the wood if you have a preference, and a rough budget. Photos of anything that inspires you help a lot; you can send them on WhatsApp with your reference number.

## 2. We confirm the details

We get back to you to agree the design, wood, finish, size and price. Prices are fixed before work begins, so there are no surprises.

## 3. An advance, then we begin

Once everything is agreed, you pay an advance by UPI and we start. You can follow your order any time on the [tracking page](/track) with your order number and phone number.

## 4. Making

The wood is selected, cut, joined, carved and finished by hand. Because each piece is made individually, the time it takes depends on the work involved. We tell you the expected time when we confirm your order.

## 5. Delivery

When the piece is ready we arrange delivery and the remaining balance. See our [delivery page](/delivery) for the areas we serve.

If you have questions at any step, [contact us](/contact). We would be glad to hear from you.
